**Yunsu Park:** In Run On Less Regional, we were able to collect data using three different methods. The first was a Geotab device which gave us good telematics data. It was very easily accessible, and we used most of that data on the Run On Less website. We also in the background had a data logger installed on the trucks, thanks to NREL, which will give us some really high-resolution data that we will use for analysis later. And then lastly, and very importantly, we also had drivers giving us data through a portal: fuel stops, as well as anytime they made a delivery or picked up a load or changed trailers.

> **Slide/Figure — Run On Less Summary Stats**
> Type: infographic text overlay
> Notes: 18 Days; 10 Fleets; 135 Days of Driving; 8.3 Average MPG; 6.0 Industry Average MPG; 2,750 Gallons Saved; $9,003,550,961 Annualized Potential Savings; 30,560,319 Potential Tons of CO2 Saved

**Mike Roeth:** The Run lasted 18 days, and with 10 fleets, there were actually 135 days of driving. The average mile per gallon across those days was 8.3 against a national average of 6.0. These 10 fleets saved 2,750 gallons over that period of time. If the 800,000 trucks driven in regional haul across North America were driven at this level of efficiency, the industry would save over $9 billion in fuel per year, and over 30 and a half million tons of CO2 would be saved.

NACFE chose to focus this year's Run on regional haul operations to demonstrate that with the right investment in technology and with skilled drivers, it is possible to achieve efficiency far above those of the average fleet.
