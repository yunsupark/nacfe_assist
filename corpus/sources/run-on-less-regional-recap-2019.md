**Mike Roeth:** The run lasted 18 days and with 10 fleets, there were actually 135 days of driving.
The average mile per gallon across those days was 8.3 against a national average of 6.0.
These 10 fleets saved 2,750 gallons over that period of time.
If the 800,000 trucks driven in regional haul across North America were driven at this level of efficiency, the industry would save over $9 billion in fuel per year, and over 30 and a half million tons of CO2 would be saved.
NACFE chose to focus this year's run on regional haul operations to demonstrate that with the right investment in technology and with skilled drivers, it is possible to achieve efficiency far above those of the average fleet.
