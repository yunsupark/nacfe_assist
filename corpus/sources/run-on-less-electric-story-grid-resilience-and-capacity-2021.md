**JESSIE LUND:**
And one thing that I think is really important to keep in mind is that these trucks are actually part of the solution in and of themselves to this resiliency issue.
You know, a big reason we're seeing more and more regulations, incentives has to do with climate change. And these trucks are the solution to the heavy-duty transportation industry addressing their emissions, particularly greenhouse gases in this case.
When it comes to resilience, it's really about ensuring that these trucks are charging at the right times, that they're acting as an asset to the electric grid as opposed to a strain during already peak critical times.
