<!-- timestamp: 02:13 -->
**Chas Mease:** Our strategy when we built this truck was to increase uptime and reduce cost. We saw that diesel trucks, especially with after treatment, had significant downtime and we wanted to solve that problem with an electric vehicle.

<!-- timestamp: 02:17 -->
> **Slide/Figure — CHAS MEASE Lonestar Specialty Vehicles**
> Type: lower-third caption

**Chas Mease:** We developed this truck to meet the most demanding applications. Many of our customers run 24/7 operations and we needed a truck to meet that. This truck will run for over 22 hours continuously before needing a charge.

**Chas Mease:** The way we could do that was 224 kilowatt hours of lithium-ion batteries. One frame mounted as you can see here on the side and the other mount on top the cab to get us at 224 kilowatt hours.

**Jason Sidders:** You can calibrate these trucks to basically be one pedal driving, which is really nice, and the closer you get to one pedal driving, the more energy capture you're going to get and the more range you're going to get out of your vehicle. And then as you're taking advantage of that region breaking, you're getting less wear and tear on your traditional service brake system, which then makes those components last longer, reduces the interval times you have on those service brakes.

**Chas Mease:** This truck uses standard protocol for charging, CCS1 on the DC fast charge side and J1772 on level two, meaning that fleets can charge this vehicle and others so long as they use CCS1 or J1772, meaning that we don't have to let the truck sit idle to charge. We can opportunity charge on lunch breaks and shift changes.

**Chas Mease:** Safety's critical in our industry. It's very important to us and to our customers and that's why we integrated a camera system into our vehicles, as you can see here. Traditional mirrors provide limited visibility especially in rain or foggy weather. These cameras provide crystal clear side views of the trailer and of the yard.

**Chas Mease:** One of the first things our operators notice right away is the increased horsepower and torque on this vehicle compared to a diesel. This Dana electric drivetrain system and the Sumo MD motor has 315 horsepower and over 2,000 foot pounds of torque.

**Ray Hancock:** It's a comfortable truck, it responds well and those are the kind of things you look at, especially in the high traffic areas that we're in. But if you got a good vehicle that maneuvers well and it keeps you relaxed during the day, that makes a huge impact on us and how we do our job and how well we do our job.
