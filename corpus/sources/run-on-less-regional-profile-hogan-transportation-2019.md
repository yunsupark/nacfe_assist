**Glen Williams:** Been at Hogan approximately 25 years. I had an older brother that drove, a lot of family members that drove, so it, it piqued my interest and I started actually driving a truck when I was about 15 years old. I've spent up until two years ago, which what I'm doing now, just backwards and forwards, but I spent most of the remaining time of the 25 years running coast to coast, running Canada, California, Florida, Georgia.

<!-- timestamp: 01:08 -->

**Glen Williams:** Drivers did help each other back in the earlier days. If a driver was on the side of the road broke down, you had four or five drivers that would try to stop and help him. Uh, it's it's changed over the years. Now I run backwards and forwards St. Louis to Kansas City every day. I may pick up loads from Olin Brass, which is metal jackets for ammunition for the military, or I may pick up a Procter & Gamble load, which is detergent, or an Anheuser-Busch load, which is a load of beer. Some days I'll take a load down and I might have two or three stops to deliver before I start coming back. So, you've got to plan.

<!-- timestamp: 01:50 -->

**Kelvin Davis:** Our Bridgeton, Missouri location is one of the busiest facilities that we do have, taking care of lease customers, we're also handling transport issues, we have dedicated that comes out of here. So this is a true 24/7 location, so there's never a slow period.

<!-- timestamp: 02:07 -->

**Mike Carroll:** Part of the reason we have the trucks come into our own terminal is because we can use our mechanics to kind of kick the tires. The vast majority of our trucking side deals with International and Freightliner. Fuel savings is always top priority.

<!-- timestamp: 02:20 -->

**Kelvin Davis:** Glen's done with his day, he'll come in on campus. He'll first visit our fuel island, and on the fuel island we have a technician that will do a bumper to mud flap inspection of that vehicle. One of the things that we really do, I think, very well here at Hogan is we take great care of our vehicles.

<!-- timestamp: 02:38 -->

**Glen Williams:** It's been a good adjustment. It really has, because over the years I missed a lot of things, you know. I missed a lot of my kids growing up, I missed a lot of birthdays, anniversaries, but now I'm home every night. I work a somewhat of a long day to most people, you know. Most people, it's an eight-hour day and they go home. I work from 12 to 14 hours, five days a week. I'm getting at the age where the home every night is more comfortable for me than staying out. I enjoy being home. I enjoy being home now.
