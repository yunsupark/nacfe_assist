**Dave Schaller:** NACFE has already pulled out a report on vans and step vans and how they performed in Run on Less Electric. As we look at medium-duty box trucks, they're performing a lot of very similar duty cycles as they're doing in-city work, and they have a lot of very similar battery characteristics, operational characteristics. So there's every reason to believe that just as vans and step vans are taking off for e-commerce, medium-duty box trucks stand a tremendous opportunity for growth.

**Dave Schaller:** Well, a medium-duty box truck can be very simplistic in having no loads going to the box. But some of the very common things that put additional loads on medium-duty box trucks are liftgates on the back ends. Additionally, many of them are refrigerated. And it's a real challenge because medium-duty box trucks are the one vehicle that's not a completed vehicle when it leaves the OEM factory for the most part. So it's going to be a level of integration and learning that the industry has to step up to.
