Also, these teams generally drive long distances and have, you know, what they'll call dedicated freight, maybe some hot freight, they call it. So less start and stop and get the miles.

**Mike Roeth:** Other opportunities with teams are that fleets employ are a trainer and a new driver. Even in those cases, we're getting more miles on that equipment and the improved fuel economy that comes with it.
