> Notes: MIKE ROETH, Executive Director, NACFE, Operations Lead, Trucking Efficiency

**Mike Roeth:**
Teams driving a truck can help efficiency in a lot of different ways.
One is the equipment utilization, so one tractor and one trailer is able to do twice the miles with two drivers.
In many cases you have a husband, wife, father, son, sometimes it's family members.
Other times it's just two drivers that enjoy being together and can drive these miles.
We've seen team driving get as much as 5,000 miles a week, 1,000 miles a day.
And what we've heard from fleets is that in team driving can actually get around 0.3, 0.4 mile per gallon improvement.
How do you get that?
Well, one is that the truck just keeps moving.
So we don't have the idle reduction needs because the truck doesn't stop to idle.
Also, these teams generally drive long distances and have, you know, what they'll call dedicated freight, maybe some hot freight they call it.
So less starting and stop and get the miles.

<!-- timestamp: 01:01 -->
Other opportunities with teams are that fleets employ are a trainer and a new driver.
So in that case, you have a team driving and, you know, again, one might sleep while the other one drives.
Or in the cases where there's some training going on, the student might be driving and the trainer might be helping them along.
Even in those cases, we're getting more miles on that equipment and the improved fuel economy that comes with it.
