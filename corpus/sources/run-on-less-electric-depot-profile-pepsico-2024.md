One of the biggest values and drivers of efficiency for the Tesla Semi is the tandem axle.

of these two allows for flexibility and range improvement and strength across both a

> **Charlotte Argue:** Senior Manager, Sustainable Mobility, Geotab

What's unique about electric trucks is that now we're looking at data around the battery
itself and the motor. So data that we look to pull includes the real-time state of charge of
that battery, so how much charge is left in the battery at any given time. And then while
that vehicle's driving or while it's charging, looking at either the energy being drawn out
of that battery for propulsion and the energy that's coming back into the battery when it's
charging or when it's regenerative breaking.
