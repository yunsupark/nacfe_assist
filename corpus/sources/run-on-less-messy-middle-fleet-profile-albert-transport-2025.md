**Henry Albert:** I have a nose cone on the trailer that also helps with the efficiency. Another piece of efficiency that's on this truck is the Hendrickson OPTIMAAX liftable axle. Because of having a lift axle, I'm only powering one differential. And the one differential I have has active lube management in it from Detroit that controls the amount of lube that's hitting the ring and pinion. But I only have one ring and pinion. I don't have an inter-axle differential and I don't have a front differential. All that leads to efficiency. Plus when I'm light and don't require both axles on the ground, it literally lifts that one right off the ground. That saves me on tire wear, fuel mileage, and also in certain applications toll roads.

**Henry Albert:** Another area of efficiency is the tires. Running a wide-base single tire, why have more tires on the ground than you need? Of course, people say, what if you blow a tire? If you run a tire monitor, which I do, it lets me know the pressure. Tires don't just blow unless they were run low, which gets them hot. And when they get hot, use heat to put a tire together, it also takes them apart. So by having a tire monitor on all my axles, I don't have to worry about that. Plus, I'm saving close to 750 pounds by having all these axles as single tires that are wide instead of a dual wheel assembly.
