<!-- timestamp: 05:27 -->
**Henry Albert:** Another area of efficiency is the tires. Running a wide-base single tire. Why have more tires on the ground than you need? Of course, people say what if you blow a tire. If you run a tire monitor, which I do, it lets me know the pressure. Tires don't just blow unless they were run low, which gets them high. And when they get high, you use heat to put a tire together. It also take them apart. So by having a tire monitor on all my axles, I don't have to worry about that. Plus, I'm saving close to 750 pounds by having all these axles as single tires that are wide instead of a dual wheel assembly.
