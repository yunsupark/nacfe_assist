**Clark Reed:** Shortly after I started uh pulling for Nussbaum, I picked up a load going to the Northwest, uh that had me going across 80 in Wyoming, which is notoriously windy. Um bill lading said 24,000 lbs where there were high wind warnings out. Um that much weight. Uh I went ahead and took the chance and and went across there. It didn't seem to be too awful bad until I got up on the mountains. Uh took a look in my right hand mirror and all I saw was the side of the trailer. Uh so I I got off as soon as I could. I waited the windstorm out. Uh come to find out later as I got into Oregon and had to go over one of their scales, there was only actually 12,000 lbs uh in the trailer in the trailer, so I should have never been on that road. I should have shut down before that because of misinformation uh given to me. I I didn't make a good decision and I didn't notice by the way it was pulling that there wasn't that much weight in the truck. Could have could have led to something much worse than it did. Nothing happened. I got through it safe, but could have been bad. So information is important whether it comes from the shipper or the receiver, uh having good information helps us make better decisions out
