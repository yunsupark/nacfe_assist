> **Slide/Figure — Driver Interview**
> Type: video clip
> Notes: On-screen lower third: Clark Reed, Nussbaum Transportation

**Clark Reed:** It was shortly after I started pulling for Nussbaum, I picked up a load going to the Northwest that had me going across 80 in Wyoming, which is notoriously windy. Bill of lading said 24,000 pounds, where there were high wind warnings out. That much weight, I went ahead and took a chance and went across there. Didn't seem to be too awful bad until I got up on the mountains. Took a look in my right-hand mirror, and all I saw was the side of the trailer. So I got off as soon as I could, I waited the windstorm out. Come to find out later as I got into Oregon and had to go over one of their scales, there was only actually 12,000 pounds in the trailer. So I should have never been on that road. I should have shut down before that because of misinformation given to me. I didn't make a good decision, and I didn't notice by the way it was pulling that there wasn't that much weight in the truck. Could have led to something much worse than it did. Nothing happened, I got through it safe, but could have been bad. So information's important, whether it comes from the shipper or the receiver. Having good information helps us make better decisions out there.
