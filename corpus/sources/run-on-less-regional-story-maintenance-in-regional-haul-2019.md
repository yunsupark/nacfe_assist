**Dave Schaller:** So there's a little bit of an opportunity here to do even better in regional haul at anticipating and supporting the fleet. One of the interesting tidbits we got from a fleet was that when they wanted to change the maximum vehicle speeds, because they had vehicles all over the country, it took between six months and a year to touch all those vehicles and make the changes. In regional haul operations, if the vehicle is back to a home base, it can be just a matter of days or weeks at most.
