We have several multi-million-mile drivers here. So the incredible amount of experience we have, they can share a lot with our audiences.

<!-- timestamp: 01:07 -->

**Mike Roeth:** But one thing that's exciting is how these seven, um, truckers and trucks, uh, mimic, uh, the industry. You know, we've, uh, we've got a couple of these trucks that are doing dedicated routes. So every day or every few days they're taking the exact same route. Uh, couple others, um, don't know today where they'll be hauling in a few days. And so it's a very irregular route, and those truckers, you know, they could be in Arizona today and, um, you know, Pennsylvania in a couple days, and then Wyoming. So, I think it's going to be really interesting to to watch that occur and to learn about how that affects their miles per gallon. And of course, we've got ton-miles per gallon, and carbon that we're saving, and some other, uh, results that that that matter with respect to the routes.

**Mike Roeth:** The data from the trucks are being streamed to us, uh, utilizing this Geotab device. So this is a harness and their telematics device. It was installed in all seven trucks, and it is the pathway to which we're getting the data. So it's vehicle speed, elevation, gallons burned, miles traveled, those sort of things. And so it's consistent across all seven trucks, and we're utilizing that to populate the runonless.com.

<!-- timestamp: 02:16 -->

**Mike Roeth:** Every day each truck's miles traveled, miles per gallon delivered, um, savings, but also their elevation, wind speed, vehicle speed, some of that data will show up for each truck for each day. Um, it will be anonymous, so you won't know which truck, um, had those numbers. But we think that's fine, because comparing to one another is is really kind of irrelevant because the routes are different with elevation changes and distances, how many starts and stops they had during the route, the, uh, wind speed is different, um, and their load is different.

<!-- timestamp: 02:49 -->

**Dave Schaller:** Mike, tell us a little bit about the different technologies on board these seven vehicles.

**Mike Roeth:** Yeah, you know, there's, uh, uh, dozens and dozens of technologies. Um, you know, we've identified 85, so, um, some of these, uh, tractors and trailers incorporate, um, many of the technologies, some are different. Uh, we've got diversity in the tires, in the trailer aerodynamics, in the powertrains from one to the other. So, you know, we were sought out to go find, uh, truck drivers and equipment that could, um, you know, deliver high MPG. And what we found is that there's no single set of specs. There's a a variety of ones that are that are happening.
