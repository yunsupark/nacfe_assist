> **Bryan Burningham:** The run on less team is here in Montreal at the former site of the 1976 Olympics. We're going to spend some time with Day & Ross that just received two brand new Lion electric vehicles. Let's go see how they're doing.

<!-- timestamp: 00:32 -->
> **Francis Lajoie:** Hello, my name is Francis Lajoie. I've been a truck driver at Day & Ross for 28 years. The biggest difference is the sound - or actually the lack of it. The quiet. It is comfortable, the design is outstanding, the visibility is great, the reliability and efficiency of the acceleration, the handling, the braking, the overall performance. After only two days on the road, I felt like I've been driving it for 10 years. I am pleasantly surprised - it's a beautiful machine.

<!-- timestamp: 00:57 -->
> **Billy Rae Rattray:** Day & Ross is actively engaging in a lot of sustainability initiatives in our corporation today, starting with speccing our fleet. So we've constantly been adopting aerodynamic packages on our trucks and trailers, investing in route optimization and telematics software to deliver freight as efficiently as possible across our network. I see the adoption of these commercial battery electric vehicles is another lever we're looking to pull to reduce our corporate greenhouse gas emissions and showcase us being leaders in our industry. We have been working through this process with our internal electrification team since 2018. So we've been analyzing and working with our OEM partners and attending different conferences and trade shows to really figure out the type of technology that works in our fleet and the proper deployment across our network.

<!-- timestamp: 01:37 -->
> **Matt Trites:** Our expectations prior to buying the trucks and our expectations now that we have the trucks really remain unchanged. Two things. Cost to operate the trucks, especially from a maintenance perspective. And secondly, sustainability. That is really what we're striving to achieve. What we've been doing for the last five years is developing medium and heavy duty electric vehicles. The Lion 6 is a fantastic vehicle. It's the first to actually be delivered in its kind in Canada. But the other thing is that it's not just a truck that's electric, it's a truck that's going to let them do what they need to do. They'll accomplish their job with a couple of things missing. There won't be any noise, there won't be the heat, there won't be the vibration. So they finish their shift feeling refreshed and rejuvenated because they didn't have that wear and tear on their body.

<!-- timestamp: 02:24 -->
> **Billy Rae Rattray:** We targeted lanes that there'll be no disruption to our operations. So we targeted lanes that are doing less than 200 kilometers a day, that we can utilize return to base charging, type of freight that's going to cube out before weighing out, so that our trucks can operate without any disruption.

<!-- timestamp: 02:39 -->
> **Patrick Kelly:** Lion for us has done a great job of bringing the vehicles here, setting them up, getting them ready to go, giving us the training, the support that we need. It's great to have that opportunity to be able to have the support of somebody that wants this to succeed.

<!-- timestamp: 02:52 -->
> **Marc-André Pagé:** All of our teams will be fully mobilized to support the Day & Ross deployment. So, Lion Energy who supported Day & Ross to expedite the installation of two ChargePoint 62.5 DC chargers. So the trucks will be able to charge in less than a couple of hours. We have an app or a card that we utilize to activate the charger. You plug it in, see the indicator lights on the truck or on the charger, or you can confirm on your app.

<!-- timestamp: 03:18 -->
> **Billy Rae Rattray:** The biggest hurdle we had to overcome to add electric vehicles to the fleet here at Day & Ross was finding the right truck. And also when we found the right truck, making sure the spec was correct. These trucks will operate in harsh conditions. It snows, it gets cold. They've got to be able to perform well and get our customers freight picked up and delivered.

<!-- timestamp: 03:40 -->
> **Matt Trites:** I'm confident that we've made a good choice here. We're really excited to get the trucks out to work.

<!-- timestamp: 03:45 -->
> **Francis Lajoie:** It's not often you can combine work with having fun. But when I'm driving this truck - I do!
