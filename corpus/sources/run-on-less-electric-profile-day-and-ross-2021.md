> **Slide/Figure — Billy Rae Rattray Identification**
> Type: lower-third caption
> Notes: BILLY RAE RATTRAY, Environmental Specialist, Day & Ross

**Billy Rae Rattray:** Day & Ross is actively engaging in a lot of sustainability initiatives in our corporation today. Starting with speccing our fleet, so we've constantly been adopting aerodynamic packages on our trucks and trailers, investing in route optimization and telematics software to deliver freight as efficiently as possible across our network. I see the adoption of these commercial battery electric vehicles as another lever we're looking to pull to reduce our corporate greenhouse gas emissions and showcase us being leaders in our industry. We have been working through this process with our internal electrification team since 2018, so we've been analyzing and working with our OEM partners and attending different conferences and trade shows to really figure out the type of technology that works in our fleet and the proper deployment across our network.

> **Slide/Figure — Matt Trites Identification**
> Type: lower-third caption
> Notes: MATT TRITES, Senior Director, Maintenance, Day & Ross

**Matt Trites:** Our expectations prior to buying the trucks and our expectations now that we have the trucks really remain unchanged. Two things: cost to operate the trucks, especially from a maintenance perspective, and secondly, sustainability. That is really what we're striving to achieve.

> **Slide/Figure — Gary Lalonde Identification**
> Type: lower-third caption
> Notes: GARY LALONDE, Vice President, Truck Sales, The Lion Electric Co.

**Gary Lalonde:** What we've been doing for the last five years is developing medium and heavy duty electric vehicles. The Lion6 is a fantastic vehicle. It's the first to actually be delivered in its kind in Canada. But the other thing is that it's not just a truck that's electric, it's a truck that's going to let them do what they need to do. They'll accomplish their job with a couple of things missing. There won't be any noise, there won't be the heat, there won't be the vibration, so they finish their shift feeling refreshed and rejuvenated because they didn't have that wear and tear on their body.

<!-- timestamp: 02:24 -->

**Billy Rae Rattray:** We targeted lanes that there'll be no disruption to our operation, so we targeted lanes that are doing less than 200 kilometers a day, that we can utilize return-to-base charging, type of freight that's going to cube out before weighing out, so that our trucks can operate without any disruption.

**Billy Rae Rattray:** We have an app or a card that we utilize to activate the charger. You plug it in, see the indicator lights on the truck or on the charger, or you can confirm on your app.

**Matt Trites:** The biggest hurdle we had to overcome to add electric vehicles to the fleet here at Day & Ross was finding the right truck. And also, when we found the right truck, making sure the spec was correct. These trucks will have to operate in harsh conditions. It snows, it gets cold. They've got to be able to perform well and get our customers' freight picked up and delivered. I'm confident that we've made a good choice here. We're really excited to get the trucks out to work.
