# 2017 Stories – Trucking Efficiency Operation

<!-- timestamp: 00:00 -->

**Narrator:** North American tractor-trailer fleets spend $105 billion per year on fuel.

**Mike Roeth:** The average tractor-trailer in the United States, sleeper kind of tractor-trailer, will do 125,000 miles a year. And at current fuel costs and current efficiency, that's around $70,000 in fuel per truck per year.

**Narrator:** The fleets can save $40 billion per year on fuel, putting that hard-earned money back in the hands of fleet owners and operators.

**Mike Roeth:** There's opportunity to save 30, 40, 50% on their fuel costs with a lot of these technologies and a lot of practices that they could adopt.

<!-- timestamp: 00:38 -->

**Bill Van Amburg:** There's technologies available right now. Why aren't fleets moving those into the marketplace? How do we get good information out to knock down the barriers and the holdbacks that are keeping fleets from taking up new technology that's available today, that has payback today, that can help them today?

**Jeff Erikson:** The aspiration is to double the fuel efficiency of the North American fleet.

**Mike Roeth:** We're operations-interested in increasing the scale and the adoption of products that already exist in the marketplace. For a tractor-trailer, we've identified 70 different technologies that are available to fleets to do that. Trucking Efficiency is going to help generate demand for these technologies.

<!-- timestamp: 01:14 -->

**Helen Marks:** We're looking to fill the gap in credible, third-party, unbiased information, and provide that information on the efficiency technologies that are available.

**Jeff Erikson:** What we've done is to develop these Confidence Reports, which are objective assessments of the fuel efficiency savings of various technologies.
