We have drivers that come in from 2:00 in the morning all the way till 10:00 a.m. and they start delivering the products to the stores. My drivers know what time they coming in in the morning. They have regular scheduled times, and they know their days off, but they do not know where they're going until they show up.

**Louis Scaruffi:** I know I'll be going to Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, or Florida, though, so...

**David Hastings:** We have a bid schedule where a driver comes in, you might have four or five of them that come in at the same time and they bid on the loads, so they get to choose where they want to go.

**Louis Scaruffi:** For most of my career, I had my work where I was off every day. But in the last few years, I've been doing some overnight runs, and actually I've I've enjoyed that. It's a little bit of change of pace after all the years I've been driving to be able to go a little bit further distance, to travel a little bit more, see a little bit more of the United States.

**David Hastings:** On certain days, I might have six or seven of them that lay over, and it's just one-day layovers. Um, but on a light night, it'll be four of them that lay over.

> **Slide/Figure — VP Identification**
> Type: lower third caption
> Notes: C&S Wholesale Grocers; CHRIS TRAJKOVSKI, VP, Transportation, Fleet Maintenance & Assets, Safety & Compliance; RUN ON LESS REGIONAL

**Chris Trajkovski:** The commitment that we made when we went into the asset replacement strategy in 2016, we wanted to also focus on the driver environment. So we've made investments in, for example, disc brakes on all components, so tractors and trailers, also making investments in collision mitigation, lane departure warning, and we've seen some of those returns come back to our organization.

<!-- timestamp: 02:57 -->

**Chris Trajkovski:** Next, we're going to be working on closing the gap between the tractor and the trailer. This is a regional setup, so a typical load coming out of here will deliver anywhere from two, three, four, on up to, you know, maybe seven stops. So every one of those stops are unique, and every one of those stops creates a challenge for the driver and potentially a conflict with the asset. So this is additional work for us to do going forward. The other avenue we want to work on is driver engagement, a better job of scorecarding MPG, not only at the local level, which they do a phenomenal job here in Hammond, but also how we do that nationally, understanding every load is going to be different, every delivery is going to be different. We just want to make sure that we're optimizing the efficiency of the asset, the technology, and that we're delivering the miles per gallon that we we think we're going to.

<!-- timestamp: 03:48 -->

**Louis Scaruffi:** I love driving these trucks. After driving these vehicles, I don't even know how I could even stand driving what I started out with. It's like night and day. There's no comparison at all.
