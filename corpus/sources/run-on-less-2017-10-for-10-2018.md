> RUN ON LESS by NACFE - NORTH AMERICAN COUNCIL FOR FREIGHT EFFICIENCY

**Narrator:** In September of 2017, NACFE conducted a comprehensive 3-week long demonstration to determine how efficient current Class 8 tractor-trailers are. We called this Run on Less. NACFE chose seven fleets, and they selected their equipment, drivers, and routes to be included in the run.

<!-- timestamp: 00:25 -->
> **Slide/Figure — Demonstration Stats Overlays**
> Type: video clip with text overlay
> Notes:
> 17 DAYS
> 99 DAYS OF DRIVING
> 50,107 TOTAL MILES
> 10.1 AVERAGE MPG

**Narrator:** The run lasted 17 days, and given rests for the seven drivers, we had 99 days of driving. Run on Less included 50,107 total miles, and these truckers averaged 10.1 MPG.

**Narrator:** And we recommend 10 actions for 10+ MPG. These are actions fleets can take to learn from the Run on Less participants and dramatically improve your efficiency.

> **Slide/Figure — 10 for 10 Infographic**
> Type: schematic / diagram
> Notes:
> 10 FOR 10: 10 ACTIONS TO ACHIEVE 10+ MPG
> LEARNING FROM NACFE'S RUN ON LESS
> - Build a Culture of Methodically Choosing Technologies
> - Keep Equipment Well Maintained
> - Implement the Right Axle Configuration
> - Provide Tools to Reduce Idle Time
> - Educate and Incent Conscientious Drivers
> - Buy All Available Tractor Aerodynamics
> - Optimize Cruise Control and Vehicle Speed
> - Use Downspaced Powertrains and AMTs
> - Embrace Low Rolling Resistance Tires
> - Adopt Appropriate Trailer Aerodynamics
> RUN ON LESS by NACFE / NORTH AMERICAN COUNCIL FOR FREIGHT EFFICIENCY

**Narrator:** Building a culture of methodically choosing technologies, keeping equipment well maintained, implementing the right axle configuration, providing tools to reduce idle time, educating and incenting conscientious drivers, buying all available tractor aerodynamics, optimizing cruise control and vehicle speed, using downspaced powertrains and AMTs, embracing low rolling resistance tires, and adopting appropriate trailer aerodynamics.
