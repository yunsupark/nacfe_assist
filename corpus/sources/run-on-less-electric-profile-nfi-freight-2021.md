> **Brett Pope:** The VNR Electric has our short bumper-to-back-of-cab, so that allows for that tight operation in city environments.
> Our energy storage system is lithium-ion batteries.
> The propulsion system for the VNR Electric consists of two electric motors with a two-speed transmission.
> These electric motors provide 340 kilowatts of power, or 455 horsepower.
> And we have a two-speed transmission because this still is a tractor, it's got to pull 82,000 pounds.
> So we have that lower first gear for launch capability, for acceleration, then we shift into second gear.
> And we have the wide operating band of the electric motors to provide that high efficiency driveline.
