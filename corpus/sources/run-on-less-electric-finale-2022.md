**Mike Roeth:** Second, the NACFE and RMI team visited all 13 electric truck sites and interviewed 91 people who are directly responsible for operating these trucks. We produced a five-minute profile video for each.

<!-- timestamp: 01:03 -->
**Mike Roeth:** And then from September 2nd through the 19th, metrics from each truck were shared on runonless.com, including details on the weather, miles traveled, and deliveries completed, the state of battery charge over the day, charging data, the levels of regenerative braking, and many others.
And for each day of the run, a story from the road was presented on items such as why drivers like electric, charging business models, and thoughts on environmental justice.

<!-- timestamp: 01:34 -->
**Mike Roeth:** In our analysis of Run on Less Electric, the 13 trucks operate in four distinct market segments of vans and step vans, medium-duty box trucks, terminal tractors, and short heavy tractor regional delivery.
5.2 million trucks in the United States and Canada operate in these segments, accounting for about 60% of all commercial vehicles.
