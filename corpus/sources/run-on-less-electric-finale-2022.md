**Mike Roeth (voiceover):** Second, the NACFE and RMI team visited all 13 electric truck sites and interviewed 91 people who are directly responsible for operating these trucks. We produced a five-minute profile video for each.

<!-- timestamp: 01:03 -->

**Mike Roeth (voiceover):** And then from September 2nd through the 19th, metrics from each truck were shared on runonless.com, including details on the weather, miles traveled, and deliveries completed, the state of battery charge over the day, charging data, the levels of regenerative braking, and many others. And for each day of the run, a Story from the Road was presented on items such as why drivers like electric, charging business models, and thoughts on environmental justice.

**Mike Roeth (voiceover):** In our analysis of Run on Less Electric, the 13 trucks operate in four distinct market segments of vans and step vans, medium-duty box trucks, terminal tractors, and short heavy tractor regional delivery. 5.2 million trucks in the United States and Canada operate in these segments, accounting for about 60% of all commercial vehicles.

**Sir Richard Branson:** Back in 2017, I was lucky enough to kick off the very first NACFE-RMI Run on Less roadshow, and I remember being asked about the future of batteries in trucks. And I said that batteries are going through tremendous improvements, and that someday we will see them hauling freight all over America. So here we are four years later, and look at the incredible progress that has been made. So well done to the teams at NACFE and RMI, and to all those who have taken part in this roadshow. Thank you for doing this openly and sharing your insights, so that the wider industry and our planet can benefit.
