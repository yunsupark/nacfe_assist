**Dan Simnick:** The aerodynamics of the truck actually is one of the things that we first started designing the LT from the ProStar. You see there's a three-piece bumper wrapping around with full chassis skirts, there's the cab extenders, the roof air fairing was designed in a wind tunnel with the extension on the top, and then you have the full aero down with the wheel covers all the way in the back. What we really designed the engine to do is meet current and future emissions standards, so there is many items on the engine that we took off or kind of engineered out that were kind of known failure modes or overly complex. So it's a fixed geometry turbocharger, there is no EGR cooler, there's no diesel oxidation catalyst, there's no seventh injector. All these things are kind of issues that other fleets and other designs of engines have had in the past, so we engineered those out, letting the engine do exactly what it wants to do, right, which is produce power. It has a very full burn of the fuel, so there's virtually no soot, and then the NOx is cleaned up on the back end, but there's no parasitic loss of recirculating exhaust gas like in other designs. So really, the design of the engine is from the ground up clean sheet to meet current and future emissions and let the engine do what it wants to do.
