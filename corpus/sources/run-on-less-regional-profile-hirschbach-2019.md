> **Slide/Figure — Speaker Identification**
> Type: Lower-third caption

**John Vesey:** We want to make sure that the consumer gets fresh food to their table every single time. We're using three different truck manufacturers right now, generally with the 15-liter engine and an automated manual transmission. We're hauling a lot more weight, so that 15-liter engine is really important to us. But the automated manual transmission is by far the best fuel economy transmission we can get. It allows the driver to focus on their pedal control and their steering, and being a lot more safe on the road.

<!-- timestamp: 02:18 -->

**John Vesey:** For the last three years, Hirschbach has earned the EPA SmartWay Excellence Award. Hirschbach buys both tractors and trailers that are SmartWay certified in order to make sure that we're up to date on the latest aerodynamic trends. We utilize the FlowBelow aerodynamic system in order to increase miles per gallon, and also to reduce road splash coming off the tires during rainy and snowy events. We do everything we can to reduce the air pocket between the tractor and the trailer. To do that, we have these cab extenders right here. They do a really good job. Hirschbach uses trailer skirts, but we are a reefer carrier, and we need to fuel the reefer unit itself. So, we have fuel holes for the drivers who fuel, and also to be able to see how much fuel he has left in his trailer.

<!-- timestamp: 03:05 -->

**John Vesey:** We use wide-base tires on both tractor and trailer. These tires, we save about 100 pounds per tire. Insulation technology inside these trailers is getting a lot better. The trailer manufacturers are always looking at ways to be able to reduce the weight, increase the insulation. The thinner we can get that insulation, the more freight we can put in our trailers, the more freight we can haul down the road in less trucks that need to haul that freight. Hirschbach's testing some solar panels on our refrigerated unit in order to keep our batteries charged when the trailer's not on, as well as keep our GPS monitoring functional and active while the battery's not in use.

<!-- timestamp: 03:43 -->

**John Vesey:** It's not just a driver out there behind that wheel. This person's a customer service representative, safety representative. They're a high-quality professional that's putting food on America's tables.
