**Dave Schaller:** So some thoughts for utility companies as we continue to move forward. Number one, get to know the fleets in your area, and don't be surprised if that fleet's headquarters are outside of your area. Number two, review your processes. How can you respond faster to truck fleets and others that are looking for quick results? Number three, you need to be aggressive. We've doubled the number of OEMs in the truck industry and half of them only produce battery electric vehicles. It's now possible to get a battery electric truck faster than you can get an internal combustion engine truck.
