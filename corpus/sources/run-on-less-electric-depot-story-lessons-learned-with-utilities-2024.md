**Dave Schaller:** So some thoughts for utility companies as we continue to
move forward. Number one, get to know the fleets in your area and don't be
surprised if that fleet's headquarters are outside of your area. Number two,
review your processes. How can you respond faster to truck fleets and others
that are looking for quick results? Number three, you need to be aggressive.
We've doubled the number of OEMs in the truck industry and half of them only
produce battery electric vehicles. It's now possible to get a battery electric
truck faster than you can get an internal combustion engine truck. There is
somebody at the utility that is looking to help you with your fleet
electrification. Engage early, engage transparently, and it's an explorative
process. Good infrastructure design serves the customer and serves the
utility.
