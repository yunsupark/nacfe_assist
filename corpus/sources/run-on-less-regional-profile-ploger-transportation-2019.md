<!-- timestamp: 01:58 -->
**Shane Sattler:** For our regional applications, we use the Volvo VNR. It's designed for regional tight spaces, great turning radius, shorter hood, shorter overall wheel base. With the D11 motor, very efficient, quiet, which the guys love. Besides the D11, they're all equipped with the I-Shift transmission, 6x2 combination.

<!-- timestamp: 02:24 -->
**Shane Sattler:** That means this axle's up the majority of the time, um, until it gets heavier weights and then the axle knows when to go down. Uh, the drivers normally don't have to hit any buttons, it does it all on its own. And, when it's a lighter load in the middle of winter, instead of trying to spread 15,000 over three axles, you're putting 15,000 on two axles. So each is carrying a bigger load, which makes for better contact on the road.

<!-- timestamp: 02:51 -->
**Shane Sattler:** We run the FlowBelow in the back as well. We've chosen the Yokohama 703L as our tire of choice. These tires are in on regional haul because of the stopping and starting more frequently, lots of turning, tight spaces. We've chose the TransTech design, um, they have the bottom skirt that runs from the dollies all the way to the front with a tandem slide and stop right there at 41.

<!-- timestamp: 03:17 -->
**Shane Sattler:** TransTech's edge top kit. We tried a couple with the whale tails and the drivers weren't using them. They said they were scared they were going to back into something with them open. This top edge kit does basically the same thing.
