> **Slide/Figure — Run on Less Electric website metrics and participant profiles**
> Type: website screenshots and video clips
> Notes: Website showing Anheuser-Busch, Day & Ross, Frito-Lay, Penske, NFI Terminal, DHL,
> and Day & Ross metrics. Shows day selection (Days 8 to 16), imperial/metric toggle,
> total miles, estimated deliveries, weather conditions, State of Charge vs speed and distance,
> Energy In (charging %, regeneration %), Energy Out (driving %, idling %), Truck Activity (inactive,
> charging, idling, driving), and Distance By Speed Range (0-19 mph, 20-39 mph, 40-50 mph, 50+ mph).

**YUNSU PARK:** We show state of charge versus speed and distance, as well as how energy comes into the battery,
the percent that's charged versus regenerated, and how energy is used, so driving versus idling.
We also have some metrics on the amount of time the truck spends charging versus inactive versus driving and idling,
as well as the amount of time it spends in various speed ranges.
Along the side of the charts, we also have some metrics on how far the truck has traveled
and the number of deliveries it's made, as well as some of the conditions it's operating in,
such as temperatures and precipitation.
We spend quite a bit of time thinking about what kind of metrics we want to show on Run on Less Electric.
It was important to really make it be intuitive and easy to understand
while still providing enough data for someone to really understand what's happening.
So a fleet or really anyone else can go in and see the kind of duty cycles and conditions they're operating in
and get a good feel for what these trucks are capable of today.
