<!-- timestamp: 04:09 -->

**Mike Roeth:** In our analysis of Run on Less Electric, the 13 trucks operate in four distinct market segments of vans and step vans, medium-duty box trucks, terminal tractors, and short heavy tractor regional delivery. 5.2 million trucks in the United States and Canada operate in these segments, accounting for about 60% of all commercial vehicles. These segments are electrifiable today, with trucks and chargers available now. If all the United States and Canadian trucks in these four market segments became electric, about 100 million metric tons of CO2 would be saved from going into the atmosphere each year. Power required to charge these trucks would be about 168 gigawatt-hours annually.
