**Mike Roeth:** Run on Less Electric is now done. And we have learned so much.

That's when say congratulations on another successful Run on Less road show.

Back in 2017, I was lucky enough to kick off the very first NACFE RMI Run on Less road show.

And I remember being asked about the future of batteries and trucks.

And I said that batteries are going through tremendous improvements and that someday we will see them hauling freight all over America.

So here we are, four years later.

And look at the incredible progress that has been made.

By coupling innovation and entrepreneurial spirit, you have shown the battery powered trucks can operate economically and sustainably in the real world and have demonstrated how the whole industry can embrace greener freight transport.

Well, thank you for doing this openly and sharing your insights.

So that the wider industry and our planet can benefit.

<!-- timestamp: 01:00 -->
**Mike Roeth:** The Run had three distinct parts.

First was the Electric Truck Bootcamp where 48 experts shared their insights and answered questions during 10 training episodes.

<!-- timestamp: 01:14 -->
**Mike Roeth:** Second, the NACFE and RMI team visited all 13 electric truck sites and interviewed 91 people who are directly responsible for operating these trucks.

We produced a five minute profile video for each.

And then from September 2nd through the 19th, metrics from each truck were shared on runonless.com.

Including details on the weather, miles traveled and deliveries completed.

As well as the state of battery charge over the day, charging data, the levels of regenerative breaking and many others.

And for each day of the run, a story from the road was presented on items such as why drivers like electric, charging business models, and thoughts on environmental justice.

<!-- timestamp: 02:01 -->
**Mike Roeth:** All in all, there are over 30 videos available to learn from these early adopting electric truck fleets.

**Selda Gunsel:** Collaboration across the industry, between energy suppliers, energy users, fleets, OEMs, government and policy makers, as well as industrial organization is critical for developing and implementing solutions.

Our partnership with NACFE on Run on Less is a perfect demonstration of our commitment to developing solutions together with our partners.

**Tim Farney:** This is about education. It's not about competition, it's about education.

And so we're excited to be a part of it.

**Amanda Phillips:** We think it really highlights what electrification can do for the industry and really take some of the the nervousness out of it for the fleet.

So they can see these trucks are out there running today and it's not just something you see on a trade show floor.
