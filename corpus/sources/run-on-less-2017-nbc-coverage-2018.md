# Run On Less 2017 – NBC Coverage

<!-- source: YouTube video, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aHU4pSYNkh4 -->

<!-- timestamp: 00:00 -->

**Male Anchor:** Seven U.S. truck fleets have set the wheels in motion for a first-of-its-kind cross-country roadshow to showcase fuel efficiency in long-haul trucking.

**Female Anchor:** Fort Wayne's NBC reporter Kent Hormann has that.

<!-- timestamp: 00:14 -->

**Kent Hormann:** The trucking industry is one of the lynchpins of commerce in this country. Facing the energy challenge head-on, this month's Run On Less program features seven drivers from several fleets demonstrating more efficiency from their rigs.

<!-- timestamp: 00:31 -->

**Mike Roeth:** The drivers, the fleets they represent, and how they're getting to 9-plus miles per gallon, is really extraordinary. And so it's our pleasure to bring it, and we think it'll have an impact because we want to influence those other truckers out there that are still at 4 or 5 miles per gallon, as well as just educate the whole consumer world and the public on what the opportunity is.

<!-- timestamp: 00:52 -->

**Kent Hormann:** As it is with other industries, technology is a huge factor with today's trucks. Better aerodynamic design, advances in engines and drivetrains, and other improvements are producing positive results.

<!-- timestamp: 01:07 -->

**Dave Schaller:** The technology is helping the industry. It's also helping the climate. The environment's better because our fuel efficiency's up and the emissions are down. Trucks are delivering everything you need, so if it costs more to deliver it, you're going to pay more for it at the store. So the more the fleets can keep the cost down, the less the appliances and food and everything else you're buying are going to cost you.

<!-- timestamp: 01:29 -->

**Kent Hormann:** The Run On Less campaign will keep on truckin' through the remainder of the month. Kent Hormann, Fort Wayne's NBC.
