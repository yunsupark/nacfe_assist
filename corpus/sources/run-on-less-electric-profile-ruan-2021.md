# Electric Profiles – Ruan

**Dave Schaller:** Welcome to Otsego, Minnesota. We're here just north of the Twin Cities to show you how Ruan and Orange EV are working together, even through cold Minnesota winters, to start truck electrification in the real commercial freight world.

<!-- timestamp: 00:31 -->

**Conrad Hanson:** My name is Conrad Hanson. I've been driving truck for approximately 44 years. My driving experience in the past has always been with a Class 8 truck. The truck I'm using currently is a yard tractor, so it's kind of hard to compare the two. But from the experience that I have had with the electric vehicle, it's an amazing change.

<!-- timestamp: 00:54 -->

**Conrad Hanson:** Originally, when I first was introduced to the idea of going to be driving an electric vehicle, I was very hesitant about the technology, but it's been a game changer for me, and I strongly recommend it, and I see it as something that's going to contribute greatly in the future.

<!-- timestamp: 01:13 -->

**Paul Jensen:** We've seen an opportunity to use new technology in various parts of our organization. If you look inside the four walls of this building, we're running 30 different pieces of electric powered equipment. We made that decision for similar reasons to making the decision to run electric powered equipment outside. It's more sustainable long term. It's a better work environment for the driver. It's less fatiguing.

<!-- timestamp: 01:33 -->

**Burt Fosse:** I was very apprehensive that the battery would work well within the winter environments of the Upper Midwest. You know, temperatures that can reach the ambient of 20, 30 below zero. And the batteries held very well. I don't remember any time that it was under 70% charged. We don't just charge it at one single point. Whenever the driver has an opportunity to do paperwork, to take a break, he plugs it in. Anytime that the truck is idle and sitting, it typically is plugged in and charging.

<!-- timestamp: 02:00 -->

**Kurt Neutgens:** This T-Series is an extension of our first vehicle. It's been in production over five years. So the batteries are a lithium-ion iron phosphate battery. With over five and a half years of experience, we've not replaced a battery in our fleet. They're all on their first battery. We make a charging system with the truck, so it's a turnkey solution.

<!-- timestamp: 02:20 -->

**Burt Fosse:** We had a little bit of time with the Orange EV folks prior to running it on the yard. Our driver spent 15 minutes with the rep, and he was ready to run it and has not needed any training since.

<!-- timestamp: 02:32 -->

**Kurt Neutgens:** Our charging systems can be hooked up to either 480-volt or 240-volt, so you don't need to have a transformer to make our systems work. Now the fast charge system, we have a standard charge that takes maybe 10 hours to charge, we have an enhanced charge, which is less than eight hours, and then we have a fast charge, which is less than two hours. And we match those to the customer needs.

<!-- timestamp: 02:55 -->

**Brad Gehring:** Our standard yard tractor diesel spec typically run 5, 6, maybe 7 years. From what we've seen of this unit's operation so far, we think we can get at least 10 years of life out of this. Ruan services a wide variety of customers throughout the country. We have over 300 operating sites. We're looking at all locations right now to see which will be a best fit for electrification. It's going to start at locations like this where there's yard tractors service. It'll move toward locations that are local, regional, and then we'll move eventually to the longer haul applications.

<!-- timestamp: 03:31 -->

**Conrad Hanson:** I've had so many instances in my past in my trucking career where I've accidentally bumped a fuel nozzle and it ruined a pair of boots or pants. With electric technology, it's just plug it in, you're good to go.

<!-- timestamp: 03:46 -->

**Burt Fosse:** The greatest thing is the opportunities of what can we do with this technology beyond what we already have. Where can we take this next? Those are the exciting things that we all have here at Ruan.
