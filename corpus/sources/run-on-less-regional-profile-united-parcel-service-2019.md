**Darin Salgado:** We have about 100 CNG and about 70 diesel. This here's the quick fuel side. This is when we pull up to the regular fuel pumps. <!-- timestamp: 02:22 -->

**Tony Coleman:** Yeah, this latest model now has air dams on the side underneath of the steps. That was not on the previous model. And then, of course, we've also on this model added the TruckWing, and get up to freeway speed, this will deploy, open up, and it closes the gap between the tractor and the trailer. And another aerodynamic enhancement that definitely improves fuel economy.

<!-- timestamp: 02:45 -->

**Tony Coleman:** Feedback from the drivers has been very positive. They can feel the difference when it engages and disengages. They notice it in the cab. The TruckWing system's going to work on both types of setups. Our local locations, we usually use the smaller trailers, and over the road is more of the larger trailers.

<!-- timestamp: 03:00 -->

**Tony Coleman:** You know, in the maintenance group, tire pressure is critical. We check our tire pressures very regularly, every inspection, make sure that the pressures are up where they need to be. That's part of fuel savings as well. We run a SmartWay tire, which improves fuel economy as well. You can effect a small percentage on just one, and then spread that across the fleet, you can see the numbers would be really big over time. So, yeah, everything that we can do to improve that economy.
