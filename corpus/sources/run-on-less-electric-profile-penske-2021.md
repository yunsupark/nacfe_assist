> Type: video clip
> Notes: DONALD DISESA, Driver, Penske

**Donald DiSesa:** I feel honored to be able to drive the electric truck, one of the first guys to drive it on my team. The truck is so quiet. Everything is smooth. It gives you time to focus on what's going on around you. With the diesel trucks, there's rattling, there's driver fatigue, things you don't even know are going on. But as soon as I got in the electric truck, I realized this is the way of the future. This is really the way it's going to go.

We feel like when we come to market next year with our series production vehicle, it is going to be the easiest to maintain on the market, and most of that we can attribute to feedback from Penske.

And with that group, we're able to engage with the fleets and understand their needs, understand how that translates to the service connections they need from Edison so that we can support their transformation.
