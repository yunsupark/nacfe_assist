> Notes: DONALD DISESA, Driver, Penske

**Donald Disesa:** My name's Donald Disesa. I drive for Penske Logistics, and I've been driving for 10 years. I feel honored to be able to drive the electric truck, one of the first guys to drive it on my team. The truck is so quiet. Everything is smooth. It gives you time to focus on what's going on around you. With the diesel trucks, there's rattling, there's driver fatigue, things you don't even know were going on. But as soon as I got in the electric truck, I realized this is the way of the future. This is really the way it's going to go.

> Notes: JED PROCTOR, Manager - Customer Consulting, Detroit eConsulting

**Jed Proctor:** One of the early things we did with Penske was we brought them into the development process, showed them Innovation Fleet, showed them what we were designing for series production, and said, "What are your thoughts?" And they gave us some really candid, good feedback that we have implemented into series production. We feel like when we come to market next year with our series production vehicle, it is going to be the easiest to maintain on the market, and most of that we can attribute to feedback from Penske.

> Notes: SIMON HORTON, Senior Project Manager, Transportation Electrification, Southern California Edison (SCE)

**Simon Horton:** In order to become familiar with the trucking industry, we've done a lot of outreach with the fleets that operate in our areas. <!-- timestamp: 02:40 -->
**Paul Rosa:** We have continuous conversations to review what we've learned from the various routes that the vehicles are running on. How is it performing in different ambient temperatures? How is it working with different loads, routes that have hills and no hills? We're trying to understand all the different ways that the vehicles can be utilized.

<!-- timestamp: 02:56 -->
**Alex Voets:** Our Freightliner eCascadia is going to go in production end of 2022 and will come out of our established truck manufacturing plant in Portland, Oregon. And when we see those vehicles come out of the production bays and all the engineering work hours that are in there, it's really exciting to see. The engineers are very excited to have these trucks roll off, because Innovation Fleet was a very novel idea at the time, but our production-ready series product is even more exciting.

<!-- timestamp: 03:25 -->
**Paul Rosa:** The drivers love it, and I think that's number one. You get drivers in a vehicle that they love driving every day, there's nothing but positives that can come out of that.

<!-- timestamp: 03:33 -->
**Donald Disesa:** My day starts at 4:00 in the afternoon. We start delivering to anywhere from 8 to 15 different customers. At the end of the day, we end up at a charging port. I pull in, plug it in, I see a green light, and I'm good to go.

<!-- timestamp: 03:50 -->
**Jed Proctor:** What I really enjoy and what I tell my family is, you know, there's no script for what we're doing today. This isn't just another powertrain that we're bringing to market where we can do all the same things we did before. We are really writing the rulebook and writing the game plan as we go along. So every day, every week, it's exciting. It's helping our customers and fleets and communities in general transition to a new way of doing business.
