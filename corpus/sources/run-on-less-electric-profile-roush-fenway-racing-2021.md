<!-- timestamp: 02:06 -->
**Jefferson Reed:**
Our Generation 1 product is based on Ford F-650 chassis. When we get the truck, it has a gasoline engine, and transmission, we take that out and convert it to our electric system. Under the hood, we have our power distribution unit, our DC-to-DC converter that takes the high voltage and converts it into low voltage for the low-voltage system that runs the cab of the truck, our charger, and our cooling system for the inside, the cab, the HVAC system, and then the cooling system for the drive motor. Over here, we have the charge port. We use the J1772 charge port and our high-voltage on-off switch.
