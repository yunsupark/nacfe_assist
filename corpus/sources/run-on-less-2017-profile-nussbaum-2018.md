**Clark Reed:** My name is Clark Reed, I drive for Nussbaum Transportation, and I've been driving for about 12.5 years. And I live in Roscoe, Illinois. Got tired of sitting behind an office desk, looking at computers. I've always loved driving, I've always loved traveling. Without trucks, the economy would fall apart within a matter of days and I don't think people realize that would happen. The types of advances I've seen since I've started, a lot of technological advances in the equipment, in the aerodynamics, in the GPS ability and the ability to actually get information when you need it. The company I run for, Nussbaum, actually rewards good driving habits and good fuel economy, so the more I save them, the more they pay me, and it just helps my check.

<!-- timestamp: 00:52 -->
**Clark Reed:** So, my 2018 Freightliner Cascadia Evolution has got a cruise control system that will allow it to back you off if you're riding up on traffic, it's going slower than you. It also gives you a display on the dashboard that lets you see how fast they're going, how far away they are from you. So that allows me to not slow down as often, not lose momentum that I've got to build back up, and that just wastes fuel. So, we've got the low bumper here that's a few inches off the ground that breaks up the airflow underneath it, keeps everything moving nice and smooth.
