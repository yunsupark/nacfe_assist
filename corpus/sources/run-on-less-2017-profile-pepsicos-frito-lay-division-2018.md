**Tommy Revell:** I'm Tommy Revell, driver for Frito Lay. I've been a driver 32 years and I'm here in Perry, Georgia. What I do every day is drive from here to Charleston, South Carolina, unload the trailer there, fill it back up with KDs, and bring it back here. Usually about a 13, 13 and a half hour trip, about 540 miles. Starting here at Frito-Lay 13 years ago, we were in cabovers. The fuel mileage, it's changed drastically. I think them cabovers got 4 and a half, 5 miles a gallon, and now we're up to some of them getting 9 and a half.

<!-- timestamp: 00:30 -->

**Ken Marko:** We've been experimenting with a lot of different technologies for our fleet. Our new powertrain systems incorporate a number of features including cruise control standard, idle shutdown systems, we've got two-speed fan drives, all geared toward trying to improve fuel economy of our vehicles, downsped transmissions, automated manuals is our standard transmission for our fleet as well. That allows drivers to focus more on driving rather than shifting.

<!-- timestamp: 01:00 -->

**Ken Marko:** We optimize our fleet by selecting aerodynamic models and maximize the use of aerodynamic features available from truck manufacturers. That includes bumpers with lower dams that help redirect airflow under the vehicle, side skirts and side extenders that help redirect the airflow around the vehicle, roof fairings, as well as aerodynamic mirrors, gap fairings help close the gap between the tractor and the trailer. We're also putting some wheel covers that help redirect air around the wheels, full side skirts to help with the aerodynamics. Another feature that we've added to our trailers in recent years has been the incorporation of trailer tails that help optimize the airflow and aerodynamic performance of our trailers.
