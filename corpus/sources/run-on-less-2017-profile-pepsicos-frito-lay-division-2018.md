I unload the trailer there, fill it back up with KDs, and bring it back here. Usually about a 13, 13 and a half hour trip, about 540 miles. **Ken Marko:** Cruise control is standard, but idle shutdown systems. We've got two-speed fan drives, all geared toward trying to improve fuel economy of our our vehicles. Downsped transmissions, automated manual transmissions as our standard transmission for our fleet as well. That allows drivers to focus more on driving rather than shifting. We optimize our fleet by selecting aerodynamic models and maximize the use of aerodynamic features available from truck manufacturers. That includes bumpers with lower dams that help help redirect air flow under the vehicle, side skirts and uh side extenders that help redirect the air flow around the vehicle, roof fairings, as well as aerodynamic mirrors. 

<!-- timestamp: 01:27 -->
**Ken Marko:** Gap fairings help close the gap between the tractor and the trailer. We're also putting some wheel covers that help redirect air around the wheels, full side skirts to help with the aerodynamics. Another feature that we've added to our trailers in recent years has been the incorporation of trailer tails that help optimize the air flow and aerodynamic performance of our trailers.
