# Electric Use Cases – Terminal Tractors

> Notes: TERMINAL TRACTORS — THE USE CASE

**Dave Schaller:** As NACFE explores electrification in the Class 8 trucking industry, one of the best initial use cases is for electric terminal tractors, or yard tractors, hostlers, jockeys...

<!-- timestamp: 00:19 -->
**Gina Lopez:** The terminal tractor market is one of those really growing markets in North America and beyond. I think one of the triggers for why the growth has accelerated so significantly has to do with the rise of e-commerce, and sort of the rise of the distribution concept.

<!-- timestamp: 00:34 -->
**Dean Newton:** They are so much more maneuverable than a day cab truck that you might try to use. It's anywhere from 3 to 10 times faster to move a trailer with a terminal tractor.

**Bill Bliem:** It works. You're in a captive environment. We opportunity charge every chance we get. We've never had a low battery or a dead battery issue.

<!-- timestamp: 00:53 -->
**Dave Schaller:** The term opportunity charging doesn't mean charging away from home base. It means taking advantage of breaks in the workers' schedules, or charging during lunch periods.

<!-- timestamp: 01:04 -->
**Burt Fosse:** We don't just charge it at one single point. Whenever the driver has an opportunity to do paperwork, to take a break, he plugs it in. Anytime that the truck is idle and sitting, it typically is plugged in and charging.

<!-- timestamp: 01:15 -->
**Joe Champa:** I'm optimistic that the EV is going to save on maintenance costs, increase uptime, and end of the day, it's going to reduce and/or eliminate carbon footprint.

<!-- timestamp: 01:24 -->
**Chas Mease:** Our strategy when we built this truck was to increase uptime and reduce cost. We saw that diesel trucks, especially with aftertreatment, had significant downtime. We wanted to solve that problem with an electric vehicle.

<!-- timestamp: 01:35 -->
**Kevin Otto:** The electrified tractor doesn't have the aftertreatment technology on it that the diesel does. Therefore, the maintenance headaches that go with the aftertreatment system are gone.

<!-- timestamp: 01:46 -->
**Ray Hancock:** It's a comfortable truck and responds well, and those are kind of things you look at, especially in the high traffic areas that we're in. But if you got a good vehicle that maneuvers well and it keeps you relaxed during the day, well that makes a huge impact on us, how we do our job and how we enjoy our job.

<!-- timestamp: 02:01 -->
**Jazmin Vasquez:** What I really do enjoy is I'm able to see everything around me. Noise, feels like it's noise cancellation in there, can't hear much from the outside. But not just that, I also enjoy the AC. I'm going to mention that, the AC is amazing, especially here in Southern California.

<!-- timestamp: 02:16 -->
**Conrad Hanson:** Originally, when I first was introduced to the idea of going to be driving a electric vehicle, I was very hesitant about the technology. But it's been a game changer for me and I strongly recommend it, and I see it as something that's going to contribute greatly in the future.
