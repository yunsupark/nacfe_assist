**Allan Dahringer:** 6x2 configuration: six wheel positions, two being the steer, two being the driven axle, and two being what I call the tag axle or the dummy axle. We have a very narrow gap between the fairing and the trailer. I believe we're down to 23, 23 and a half inches. In the event you have to make a U-turn, we have a stripe on the trailer that's just prevention of damaging that cab extender right there. We have a stripe right here, a vertical stripe, which we put on every trailer. So if you do make a 90-degree turn, you can still see that stripe in your mirror. If you go anywhere past that, or if you lose sight of that stripe, that's when a decision needs to be made: do I continue with this U-turn, or do I stop and seek a different route?
