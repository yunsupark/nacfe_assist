**Mike Roeth:** I'm often asked, why do you use miles per gallon instead of freight moved per gallon, things like ton miles per gallon or maybe cubic miles per gallon? And it's a very good question, and we do want to use freight hauled per gallon, because that's the important measure here. What if you fill the trailer up and haven't grossed out, or aren't at the legal limit of 80,000 pounds? Maybe you're hauling ping pong balls, or in the case of Run On Less, you're Frito-Lay and you're hauling snacks. These are very light loads.

<!-- timestamp: 00:32 -->

**Mike Roeth:** So in this particular case, Frito-Lay has decided to use a drop frame trailer. It's a specialty trailer produced by Kentucky Trailer, and it gives 11% more space in the trailer to haul freight. So, filling up these trailers is an important part of efficiency, whether you're grossing out at the weight or cubing out, as Frito-Lay has as shared here. I'd like the whole industry to keep thinking about freight efficiency rather than just miles per gallon.
