**Mike Roeth (Executive Director, NACFE):** One of the things we look at is daycab production. 45% of the Class 8 tractors that go over the road produce now are daycabs. But also we're seeing that more sleepers are being used in daycab operations. So if you really study the 10 duty cycles in Run on Less Regional, there's really only one or maybe two that should have a sleeper, but there's really I think four that are operating sleepers. So why is that? They may have situations where the driver needs to stay out overnight.

**Mike Roeth (Executive Director, NACFE):** So we saw that at CNS where some of those truckers will actually spend a night on the road, but stay at a hotel.
**David Hastings (Fleet Manager, C&S Wholesale Grocers):** On certain days I might have six or seven of them that lay over, and it's just one-day layovers, um, but on the light night, it'll be four of them that lay over.
**Louis Scaruffi (Driver, C&S Wholesale Grocers):** It's been good for me. I I've enjoyed it. I know some drivers, they they just, you know, frown on having to stay overnight, but to me, that's part of driving.

**Kerry Stritt (VP, Fleet Services, C&S Wholesale Grocers):** Majority of our drivers are home every night, or at most one night a week they stay out.
**Mike Roeth (Executive Director, NACFE):** Conversely, some of the fleets operate only sleepers, and maybe that's because most of their trucks at that particular center do multiple nights out. But they have one or two drivers like Dustin with J.B. Hunt, where he comes home every night, but they want to have one common truck spec for that location and they use a sleeper for that. That also gives them some flexibility to use that sleeper in other routes if they need to when Dustin may not be there, or they don't have enough of those daycab routes.

<!-- timestamp: 01:30 -->
**Mike Roeth (Executive Director, NACFE):** So predominantly, daycabs resonate with regional haul. Daycab is cheaper. They have a lower weight, so they're good for the duty cycle, but can't serve the whole market. And so we'll probably see daycabs versus sleepers in regional haul for some time.
