> Notes: DERIK WILSON, HD Infrastructure & Charging Manager, Volvo Trucks North America

**Derik Wilson:** Driver behavior is the biggest thing. These trucks are really torquey off the line, so teaching them how to operate that truck in the most efficient way possible, also capturing regeneration. The stalk for the regen has three modes, so knowing when you're capturing that inertia back into the batteries is the biggest thing on extending the range. And route optimization, obviously driving at moderate speeds, both highway and city, will help with the range as well.

> **Slide/Figure — Lower Third**

> Notes: SASHKO STUBAILO, Co-Founder / CTO, Flipturn

**Sashko Stubailo:** Two of the earliest things that 4Gen found really helpful, one was understanding the efficiency of their vehicles so they could coach their drivers to, you know, lay off the brakes sometimes, to use regenerative braking. Another thing was just in real time as their fleet is driving around, understanding which vehicles are charged to which level so that they can pick the right ones for the right routes. And then, of course, they would be spending a lot more money on electricity if they weren't able to use our charger management functionality as well. For example, between 4:00 and 9:00 PM it might be twice as expensive as in the morning, and so our system automatically selects the optimal time to charge that will save you the most money.

**Hani Harawi:** We can look at the performance, understanding how much range are these trucks providing so that utilization is maximized because these are expensive vehicles and you don't want to minimize how much you're using them because you're worried about running out of range.
