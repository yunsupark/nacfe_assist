**Dean Magistrale:** We estimate, as it's a new technology, but we're curious if we'll ever put a set of brake pads on one of these tractors. Regenerative part slows the truck down so well that really a driver's only using the brakes for the last 60 feet, 70 feet of stopping. Based upon my past experience, a municipal haul tractor hauling beverages in downtown Los Angeles would wear out a set of brakes every two to three years. So, regenerative braking is saving an awful lot of wear and tear on the braking system.

<!-- timestamp: 03:26 -->

**Michael Gallagher:** The infrastructure is a lot of different pieces: getting the utility connection, dealing with any supply chain issues to get the switchgear in here and the transformers. We were fortunate enough to partner with Prologis Mobility. They build out all of the sites that we have. So at the end of the day, they're responsible, they own the chargers, they install the chargers, they maintain 'em, uh, they get the utility connection here, they administer all of the credits that we have, and it's a really kind of turn-key operation for us. That was the only way that we could scale it. You know, I did the first project myself and realized quickly that if we wanted to scale, that wasn't the method to do it. So, partnering with Prologis Mobility and and Volvo from an OEM standpoint is is really got us to where we're at today.

<!-- timestamp: 04:03 -->

**Andy Manos:** When we first spoke with them, it was, "Hey, we have vehicles coming and we need infrastructure." The kind folks at Volvo beat everybody's expectations and delivered more trucks faster than anyone had anticipated, right? And we had to meet 'em with the infrastructure. So when we first started talking, they were pretty far along and said, "Great, here's our timeline. This is when we need to get the project built." About nine months later, we had this energized, we had the sister site to it energized, and we were rolling about 40 trucks every single day. That's an outlier. Most of our partners that we've been speaking with are in early stages: "Hey, we're thinking about transitioning," or "There's a regulatory pressure," or "There's an incentive opportunity that we want to leverage. How can we work together to ensure that either I don't have vehicles sitting, or I don't have infrastructure sitting? How can we match up to make those happen?" It is a full turnkey installation. All the operations and maintenance and everything is rolled into that price per kilowatt-hour, so that this company doesn't have to think about it again. They just show up, plug in, and charge.
