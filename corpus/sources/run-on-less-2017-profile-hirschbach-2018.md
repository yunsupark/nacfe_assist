> Notes: BRAD LONG | HIRSCHBACH | ON THE ROAD 12 YEARS | INDIANA

**Brad Long:** My name is Brad Long. I work for Hirschbach. I've been driving for 12 years and I'm from Indiana. Over my career, I've driven approximately 1.3 million miles. Fuel efficiency is important to me because that's more miles that I'm able to drive before I need to stop and get fuel again, and also that I receive a fuel bonus for saving fuel. The greatest highlight for me on the road are actually those times that I get to have my family with me. They get to see what my life is like here, and they get to see the importance of the things that we do to get food to people.

**John Vesey:** The whole goal of our green fleet strategy is to reduce carbon emissions while increasing fuel economy. All of our trucks, all of our equipment's all late model. Keep our trucks within 3 to 4 years old, keep our trailers within 5 years old, reduce our maintenance costs, it really improves our fuel economy, which we pass down to the lease operators, to the owner-operators, and to the company drivers as well.
