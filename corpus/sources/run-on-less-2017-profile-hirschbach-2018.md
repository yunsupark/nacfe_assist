**Brad Long:** My name is Brad Long. I work for Hirschbach. I've been driving for 12 years and I'm from Indiana. Over my career, I've driven approximately 1.3 million miles. Fuel efficiency is important to me because that's more miles that I'm able to drive before I need to stop and get fuel again, and also that I receive a fuel bonus for saving fuel. The greatest highlight for me on the road are actually those times that I get to have my family with me. They get to see what my life is like here, and they get to see the importance of the things that we do to get food to people.

<!-- timestamp: 00:46 -->
**John Vesey:** The whole goal of our green fleet strategy is to reduce carbon emissions while increasing fuel economy. All of our trucks, all of our equipment's all late model. Keep our trucks within three to four years old, keep our trailers within five years old. Reduces our maintenance cost. It really improves our fuel economy, which we pass down to the lease operators, to the owner operators, and to the company drivers as well.

<!-- timestamp: 01:10 -->
**Brad Long:** This is the Bendix Wingman with Adaptive Cruise Control. It helps me keep a safe distance from other vehicles while keeping my fuel efficiency great. We also use tire pressure sensors. We have these on all wheels on the truck. Michelin low rolling resistance tires on all of our trucks and trailers. Late model reefer units, well maintained for good fuel efficiency. Hirschbach continues to improve its fuel efficiency by putting trailer skirts on all of our trailers, and I can tell the difference on my bonus check. Because of the weight of the frozen food that we haul, we use light weighting aluminum wherever possible.
