> **Slide/Figure — Run Statistics & Savings**
> Type: text slide / summary data
> Notes: RUN ON LESS NACFE; 18 Days; 10 Fleets; 135 Days of Driving; 8.3 Average MPG; 6.0 Industry Average MPG; 2,750 Gallons Saved; $9,003,550,961 Annualized Potential Savings; 30,560,319 Potential Tons of CO2 Saved

**Mike Roeth:** The run lasted 18 days, and with 10 fleets, there were actually 135 days of driving. The average mile per gallon across those days was 8.3, against a national average of 6.0. These 10 fleets saved 2,750 gallons over that period of time. If the 800,000 trucks driven in regional haul across North America were driven at this level of efficiency, the industry would save over 9 billion dollars in fuel per year, and over 30 and a half million tons of CO2 would be saved. NACFE chose to focus this year's run on regional haul operations to demonstrate that with the right investment in technology and with skilled drivers, it is possible to achieve efficiency far above those of the average fleet.

**Mike Roeth:** Over the course of the Run On Less, NACFE also discovered that high efficiency requires a commitment from both fleet leadership and drivers. Big data and connectivity can be used to further optimize for each route. Diversity in duty cycles makes it essential that fleet managers understand the nuances of their business so that they can spec vehicles properly and coach drivers in efficient driving techniques. The expansion of regional haul will help attract and retain drivers as drivers get home on a more regular basis. And because of its return-to-base operation, regional haul is ideal for alternative-fuel vehicles, especially battery electric trucks.

**John Walters:** Technology is going to be a huge factor in efficiency moving forward. Education is also a huge factor. And these fleets involved in Run On Less are a clear demonstration that you can make a big impact on your fuel efficiency today.
