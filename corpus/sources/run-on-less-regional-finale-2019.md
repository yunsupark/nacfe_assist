<!-- timestamp: 04:03 -->
**Mike Roeth:** The run lasted 18 days. With 10 fleets, there were actually 135 days of driving. The average mile per gallon across those days was 8.3 against a national average of 6.0. These 10 fleets saved 2,750 gallons over that period of time. If the 800,000 trucks driven in regional haul across North America were driven at this level of efficiency, the industry would save over $9 billion in fuel per year and over 30.5 million tons of CO2 would be saved.

<!-- timestamp: 04:46 -->
**Mike Roeth:** NACFE chose to focus this year's run on regional haul operations to demonstrate that with the right investment in technology and with skilled drivers, it is possible to achieve efficiency far above those of the average fleet. Over the course of the Run on Less, NACFE also discovered that high efficiency requires commitment from both fleet leadership and drivers.

<!-- timestamp: 05:14 -->
**Mike Roeth:** Big data and connectivity can be used to further optimize for each route.
