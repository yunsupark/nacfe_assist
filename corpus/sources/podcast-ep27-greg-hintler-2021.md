**Greg Hintler:** It's a fantastic question. <!-- timestamp: 15:29 -->
**Greg Hintler:** Yeah, vehicle-to-grid integration—Gridworks, one of the organizations in the space, an NGO that's working on vehicle-to-grid integration, has defined VGI as the way how we drive electric while driving down the cost and emissions of electric grids. So vehicle-to-grid integration includes managed charging and demand response use cases, V1G, so that's unidirectional charging. And then V2G, those are the use cases in which vehicle batteries discharge power back onto the grid. So that's V2G, so bidirectional energy flow. To give you some examples here: V1G, so the unidirectional flow of power, that's what we do when we respond to time-of-use tariffs. So when we control charging such that the fleet is charged at the time when the electricity is the cheapest, and that's something we and other companies are doing today. We're doing this with our software called ChargePilot that helps us control charging at the largest transit fleet in Metro St. Louis and at over 300 sites across the globe. And then you got V2G, so that's the bidirectional charging of electric vehicles. And that's where we're only at the beginning of the industry right now. There are still some technology and policy challenges, but the idea here is that we can both charge a vehicle and discharge from the vehicle back onto the grid. And an example here is what we're doing in Porto Santo. Porto Santo is a small island in the middle of the Atlantic. And there, at the Mobility House, we charge vehicles on that island when there's a lot of renewable power being generated, so when the wind blows and the sun shines on the island. And then when there is a cloud going over the island and blocking the sun or the wind [drops], [inaudible in source captions] power back from the vehicles onto the grid to help stabilize the grid there. You asked why should we care about this? Why should fleets today and trucking today, why should we care about this? And the general value proposition here is VGI helps stabilize the grid while also being able to add more renewables to the power. So it's a societal benefit, but for the fleet owner specifically, there are many benefits as well. So there will be schemes that allow vehicle owners to generate additional income through discharging when the demand is at its peak on a system. And then it also provides backup power and resiliency options for a fleet or for a depot in situations where, for instance, in California when you have power shutoffs due to the wildfires, or as we've seen last fall in Texas when there are cold spells.

**Greg Hintler:** Well, I think for fleets, we always tell fleets to do five main things. That's: plan early; work with an organization to look at your charging infrastructure to prepare for the future of your fleet. And that will allow you to reduce your electricity costs and reduce your capex expenditures. Look for interoperable technology; you don't want to get locked in with a specific type of technology today, especially as we're so early on in the industry. We tell them, even if you have your first vehicles in operation, the journey hasn't ended; like, you continually want to optimize further even once you have vehicles in operation. We tell folks, "Hey, look at different ownership models; if the upfront cost scares you, there are alternatives to that." And then finally, build for the future. From technology to policy, everything is evolving so quickly; you might want to add more vehicles and chargers in the future, you want to maybe participate in vehicle-to-grid integration schemes in the future. So make sure that you're set up for that from the get-go. And now to your question around what for the general public, how to think about electrification: well, I say, go to your dealer, whatever dealer you're working with, whatever brand you're interested in—I think by now almost every major brand has some exciting electric vehicle out there. And my last point goes, I think, to consumers, but also fleets and industry professionals who are thinking about all this, is: have fun doing all of the above, because we are building a new world and that's just incredibly exciting, and that's a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.
