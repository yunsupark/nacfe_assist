Sacramento is a production plant, so we haul a lot of water. That's one of the main things that we do. Typically from the Sacramento plant, we go down to Hayward, and from Hayward, we bring back loads to smaller plants such as Benicia or the Modesto plant. And then from there, instead of coming back to Sacramento empty, we haul for other companies.

<!-- timestamp: 01:56 -->

> **Slide/Figure — Speaker Lower-Third**
> Type: lower-third caption
> Notes: PEPSICO; ADRIAN RODRIGUEZ, Fleet Sustainability Manager; RUN ON LESS REGIONAL

**Adrian Rodriguez:** ...Light-weighting to Pepsi is extremely important because it allows us to carry extra payload, pull miles out of the system, and positively impact our consumer. Our lightweight trailers have composite floors to further reduce the weight and increase payload. We've also added super single tires with aluminum wheels. We've also got onboard scales on our tractors and our trailers. This allows us to even further optimize our payload by making sure that we're getting every last pound on the trailer. 

<!-- timestamp: 02:23 -->

**Adrian Rodriguez:** Another option we've added to our trailers are lightweight composite doors. We've reduced the interior lining to pallet height. We load beverages, we don't necessarily ever go above that amount. It's a little bit of added weight reduction.

<!-- timestamp: 02:37 -->

**Ken Marko:** Some other features that we've incorporated in our vehicle include powertrain optimization. So we've incorporated automated manual transmissions to help drivers operate the vehicle more efficiently, an adaptive cruise control system with collision mitigation. We've also combined it with a predictive cruise control, which allows us to control the vehicle speed as we're going up and down hills and optimize for fuel efficiency as well.

<!-- timestamp: 02:59 -->

**Adrian Rodriguez:** From an aerodynamic standpoint, all our transport trailers come equipped with nose cones, along with low rolling resistance tires on both the trailer and the tractor. We've also added trailer skirts to all of our transport trailers. As many fleets, we use TrailerTails for improved fuel economy to try to overcome some of the challenges that we've seen. We've added the flag to make sure that the driver can see it in his rearview mirror and know when the unit is deployed. All our trailers come equipped with automatic tire inflation systems, making sure that we've got the optimal tire pressure for fuel economy.

<!-- timestamp: 03:33 -->

**Ken Marko:** We're committed to improving the fleet. We're committed to investing in safety technology to make sure that our roads are safer for everybody, and make sure we're introducing sustainable technologies in our fleets to improve fuel efficiency and reduce the amount of fuel we use to deliver our products as well to the market.
