**Dave Schaller:** If you're going from a high altitude city and your first run of the day is downhill, you don't want your vehicle fully fueled.

**Kevin Otzenberger:** You don't want to charge it all the way up to 100% because then you won't have that regenerative braking. You can strategically charge it to, let's say, 80 or 85% or whatever is needed in this case. You can parameterize the truck, you can actually program a charge before you leave your shift at the end of the day to say that I want my truck to be 85% full at 8:00 tomorrow morning, and I want the cabin to be 72 degrees. And while the truck is plugged into a charger, it'll make sure that it's in exactly those conditions when the driver arrives in the morning. These are the kind of things that you can plan, and the more reliable and predictable your route is, the easier that is to do.
