The objective of this Run On Less here in September of 2017 is to show what the best trucks and the best drivers that are currently available can do. And we're seeing high fuel economy, some that approach the levels of the DOE SuperTruck programs from a couple of years ago. In 2009, the Department of Energy announced and invested in four programs to improve the efficiency of Class 8 heavy-duty tractor-trailers.

<!-- timestamp: 00:36 -->

**Mike Roeth:** And four teams took off and took that challenge. One was led by Cummins with Peterbilt, another with Navistar, Daimler, and Volvo, and they all delivered incredible vehicles. We've seen that in the press. Those vehicles performed and operated in real-world testing conditions through the duty cycle assumed by the engineers at those companies, and they showed efficiency in the 11 to 13 mile per gallon range.

<!-- timestamp: 00:59 -->

**Mike Roeth:** First of all, weight and payload matter. The DOE SuperTruck programs had a 65,000-pound expectation or design limit. And so the trucks were designed for that heavy load, and the trucks operating in Run On Less are at a lower level of weight generally. Also, the SuperTruck programs were designed with a pretty difficult duty cycle for their MPG demonstrations, which challenged the trucks and the drivers to get to the high levels.

<!-- timestamp: 01:25 -->

**Mike Roeth:** Some of the technologies incorporated in the SuperTrucks don't exist in these current trucks. So some of the items like waste heat recovery and some of the other electrification technologies that were put in the demonstrators from the DOE SuperTruck program are not showing current payback for fleets or for manufacturers to bring them to production.

<!-- timestamp: 01:44 -->

**Mike Roeth:** So this level of Run On Less performance of currently available technologies is very impressive for what they're doing with the equipment that the OEMs have given them, and kudos to the OEMs for so quickly bringing the SuperTruck designs into their current production.
