> Notes: BILL BLIEM, SVP, Fleet Services, NFI Industries

**Bill Bliem:** It works, you're in a captive environment. We opportunity charge every chance we get. We've never had a low battery or a dead battery issue. And maintaining the trucks and keeping them charged, that's done um we usually use it intermittently through their breaks and lunches, depending on how the voltage of the truck is. At times uh may be a little lower so they may need to take an extra break, but it's all done breaks and lunches and in between shifts.

**Marco Molina:** Currently our charging ports are situated next to the break rooms on the campus. Some are technically in the back of the buildings or in the front of buildings, and we've changed some stuff infrastructure-wise, like added doors to gates to make it easier for our drivers to get to the charging ports and to the trucks. That helps with efficiency in keeping everything charged as much as possible.

**Gina Lopez:** NFI specifically is one of those fleets that really can test their paces. They have some of the most aggressive applications that we've seen of our deployed fleet, um driving the longest distances, reaching the higher speeds, um the number of picks and drops that they're doing in a given shift, they're on the very high extreme level. So, it's allowing us to then refine our product design so that we make sure that we're taking care of those top levels or those top tiers of uh customer operations.

**Dean Newton:** Energy on board could be lessened. They need to do the opportunity charging not anyway. Uh so I'd say that was probably the biggest learn that we've got.
