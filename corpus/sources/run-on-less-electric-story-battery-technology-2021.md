**Rick Mihelic:** There's a new idea every week in media about some new great battery technology that's going to go the distance, be half the price, get the job done. **Matt Wetta:** The technology available, and in particular the battery technology available, its capability in terms of energy storage and its cost to do so, has been falling pretty steadily for quite a few years and is now beginning to get to the point where it can really be cost-competitive or even better than diesel in the right applications. As better battery technology comes to market, we'll continue to expand the range of applications which are available with an EV.

**Sir Richard Branson:** Back in 2017, I was lucky enough to kick off the very first NACFE RMI Run On Less roadshow.
