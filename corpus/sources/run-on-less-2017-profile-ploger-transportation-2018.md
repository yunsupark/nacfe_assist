**Joel Morrow:** My name's Joel Morrow. I drive for Ploger Transportation. I'm based in Bellevue, Ohio, and I've been driving for about 30 years.

<!-- timestamp: 00:20 -->
**Joel Morrow:** In my career I've driven well over 4 million miles. On a daily basis I travel about 530 miles doing multi-stop furniture deliveries. Fuel efficiency is important to me because it's a reflection of how well I do my job. There's no escaping that final number and and it's a direct reflection of how well you operate your equipment. Education and technology is is going to be key to succeeding in this business going forward.

<!-- timestamp: 00:46 -->
**Joel Morrow:** What we're doing different is we have an 11-liter engine instead of a 13 or a 15, so we have less rotating mass in the engine when you get into traffic or you're bringing the truck up to speed, results in greater fuel efficiency.

<!-- timestamp: 01:00 -->
**Joel Morrow:** This is the Wall-Bash Drag Reduction System that kind of lets pressure equalize from the outside and the underneath of the trailer. It's something new for us, but initially the fuel economy readings from this have been just outstanding. This is the Wall-Bash AeroFin XL. It's very easy to deploy. It automatically opens and closes with the lock rods on the door, so there's no extra steps for the driver to go through. It looks like we're gaining about 3 to 4 tenths of a mile a gallon on this with this particular tail.

<!-- timestamp: 01:30 -->
**Joel Morrow:** The tires that I like to use on my truck are low rolling resistance tires in all the free rolling positions that really helps to improve the fuel economy. And we're finding that the the higher rolling resistance tire in the drive position works better for us in our 6x2 applications.

<!-- timestamp: 01:47 -->
**Joel Morrow:** This is my electronic log. I think it's one of the greatest things. It forces you to manage your time and it forces you to become more efficient.

<!-- timestamp: 01:54 -->
**Joel Morrow:** This is my OBD reader. I can bring up all kinds of different little boxes to see what's going on with the engine. I like to watch my fuel economy, the engine horsepower, and the engine torque.

<!-- timestamp: 02:05 -->
**Joel Morrow:** This is my pedal coach device. This is the in-cab portion of the device and it's basically a very simple swing arm that lets the driver visualize fuel flow through the injectors as he's pressing the pedal. So the driver understands what his interaction with the fuel pedal is doing and it is a very nice device to help increase fuel economy.

<!-- timestamp: 02:26 -->
**Joel Morrow:** Ploger Transportation is ready to Run on Less.
