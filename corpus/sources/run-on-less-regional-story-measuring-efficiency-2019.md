Measuring efficiency in regional haul is a pretty big challenge. There's a lot of different duty cycles and drive cycles with these trucks. Some are hauling very heavy, they go out at a full 80,000 pounds and have a diminishing load over the day, where others go out at maybe 20,000 pounds payload and maybe 65,000 GVW and and go all the way out, drop that trailer, pick up another one and come back. So there's a lot of differences in in how they operate these trucks. So sharing their efficiency is a pretty big challenge.

<!-- timestamp: 00:37 -->
Not only will we measure miles per gallon and ton miles per gallon as you've seen NACFE do before, but the fact that we've got both telematics and working with national labs to provide data recorders, we're going to have far more data and deep dive into that data as we go through.
