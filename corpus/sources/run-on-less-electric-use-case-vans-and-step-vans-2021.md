**Chris Nordh:** We've really reinvented the step van. And what I mean by that is that with the electric drivetrain, you're able to really look at the architecture of the vehicle completely differently. So the floor of this vehicle is 10 inches lower to the ground than a traditional step van, which means that it takes two steps instead of three steps to get in or out of the vehicle. Now, that can be very significant for somebody that is making 200 stops a day. And the other aspect with electric vehicles is obviously the one-pedal driving. We have a very aggressive regen setting on these vehicles, which means that they decelerate quite aggressively and come to a complete stop without anybody needing to touch the brake pedal. That's great from a maintenance perspective as well as from the driver comfort perspective.
