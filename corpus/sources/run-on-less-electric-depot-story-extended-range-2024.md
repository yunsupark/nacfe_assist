**Mike Roeth:** Range is one of the biggest challenges we have with battery electric trucks. A lot of people think that the range is only how far a vehicle can go on a charge. **Dean Magistrale:** The opportunity charge is when the driver returns from his first run on a Generation 1 truck. We like to get between 30 minutes to an hour of charge, get the battery up to about 75 or 80%. So, they can make another port run.
