> **Beau White:** An average day for me is probably at least 10 hours. I run around 542 miles a day. I go out to Livingston, Alabama, which is 17 miles this side of Mississippi. About four and a half hours over there, four and a half hours back. A 30 minute break. I meet at a truck stop, I meet one of our Dallas team trucks coming out of Dallas, two guys.
> 
> **Kerry Stritt:** We try to get our road drivers 450 to 500 miles a night. And being a next day carrier, we have to do things rapidly. And we'll have drivers meet at a midpoint and swap loads. That way drivers can get back to their home base and they don't have to stay out on the road overnight.
> 
> **Jim Boyd:** At a midpoint we could have two drivers or more drivers meet, mix and match the 28-foot trailers and create destination loads that are advantageous to our operation.
> 
> **Slide/Figure — Southeastern Freight Lines / Jim Boyd**
> Type: lower-third caption
> Notes: SOUTHEASTERN FREIGHT LINES, JIM BOYD, Manager, Fleet Technical Services

<!-- timestamp: 02:01 -->
> **Kerry Stritt:** As an LTL company, we don't tend to max out on weight. We cube out. So we want to run pups. And the ideal combination is a single axle tractor or a 4x2.
> 
> **Beau White:** I love doubles. Actually, it's a comfortable ride, especially with a single axle truck. I have pulled twins with a tandem truck and it'd cost about 10 to 12 gallons more of fuel with a tandem truck.
> 
> **Kerry Stritt:** We have a full shop here in Atlanta, and this is one of our largest. We have about 45 technicians. And we do all of the maintenance on all of our equipment. Uh, we can rebuild engines, we do all of the preventive maintenance, same thing on our trailers.
> 
> **Jim Boyd:** The biggest piece for fuel economy really is the marriage of the engine and the transmission. In this vehicle we've got a 15-liter common rail injection engine married to an automated manual transmission, both of which are ECM controlled and both of which talk to one another.
> 
> **Beau White:** A trailer that's got full aerodynamic, low-rolling resistance tires, automatic tire inflation systems, all of those pieces add up to create the total aerodynamic package. But some of it is operationally based.
> 
> **Jim Boyd:** If you look at the trailer that we're looking at today, it's got a full decking system, a modular decking system that we can tailor ourselves. Our load average goes up, we put less vehicles on the road which is reduces congestion, our mileage goes down which reduces our exposure to accident and wear and tear on the vehicles just goes down in general.
> 
> **Beau White:** Like the first time I came to work here, it was a, it was a Volvo white. It was a metal hood that opened up on both sides. But I was working for Southeastern Freight Lines, so I was happy. We do a good job training our drivers here. I'm proud of that because I think we've got one of the best safety records in the, in the country. Because I still love driving a truck. That's the biggest part of my life, really. It's provided for my family.
