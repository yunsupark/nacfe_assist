**Narrator:** Trucks operate in the real world. The real world has weather, has traffic, it has mountains, deserts, and cities. It has delays, it has hours of service rules, inspections, permits, and violations. The real world has downtime for these trucks, things break and get repaired. It has costs, profits and losses, and real competition. The real world has real drivers, real payloads. The real world is really tough on trucks.

<!-- timestamp: 00:40 -->

**Narrator:** These stories from the road is our daily insight into this real world. Over the course of the next 18 days, we will provide you visibility into the reality of the current operation of battery electric trucks, their hopes, excitement, challenges, and lessons learned from these early adopting fleets.
