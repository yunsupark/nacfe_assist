> **Slide/Figure — WAREHOUSE PLANNING: FUTURE PROOFING**
> Type: text callout banner
> Notes: WAREHOUSE PLANNING - FUTURE PROOFING

**Jennifer Wheeler:** Fleets also need to consider future proofing as they think about where and how many chargers to add at their facility as they continue to grow their electric fleet. It's less expensive to tear up your parking lot once instead of every time you add a new vehicle.

**Bill Bliem:** It is an issue when you're making a million-dollar investment in charging infrastructure into a facility that you don't own the real estate.
