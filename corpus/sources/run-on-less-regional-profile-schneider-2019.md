**Steve Jarosinski:** Full length cab extenders and then uh basically trying to minimize that gap between the tractor and trailer and then also even tucking the trailer up as far as we can while still meeting weight requirements. Trying to smooth out the truck here. Uh aerodynamic wheel covers with our aerodynamic inter-drive axle panels front and rear and then finally onto the trailer for the side skirts. Low front bumper, lower ground clearance here helps us control the air flow underneath the truck, which is very important for fuel economy.

<!-- timestamp: 02:30 -->
**Steve Jarosinski:** Obviously, MPG isn't just about driving, it's the total fuel you consume. So idle is a big part of that and to get away from some idle uh we will use either diesel APUs or battery powered APX systems. Regional is becoming more and more popular, so we do have specialized specs that kind of balance that need of city versus strictly highway to make them as efficient as possible. Regional haul has its fair share of challenges that over the road may not, uh but safety, on-time delivery, and then getting the driver uh their promised time at home are things that do very well.

Safety is paramount, be patient.
