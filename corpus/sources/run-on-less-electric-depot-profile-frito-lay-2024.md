**Mike Roeth:** Here we are in New York City, specifically Queens. We're at a Frito-Lay ProLogis site where they're completely transforming this from gasoline trucks to electric trucks in only nine months with these Ford E-Transits.

<!-- timestamp: 00:38 -->

**Adam Buttgenbach:** As part of our sustainability initiatives at PepsiCo, we launched pep+ in 2021, focused on both people and planet, and that's really the strategic transformation of our fleet and supply chain. And so being able to have these aggressive goals means that we can't just talk about it, we've got to turn talk into action.

**Jordan Ratcliff:** We're able to move fast at Queens because of the duty cycle here. These vehicles are traveling in a really compact urban area, and because of that, they don't have incredibly high mileage. So, we're able to utilize lower charger ratios than 1:1 that's required at other sites. And because of that, the demand on the utility upgrades is just a lot smaller.

<!-- timestamp: 01:18 -->

**Adam Buttgenbach:** So, this actually started at ACT Expo in 2022. We were meeting with ProLogis, who shared in a lot of our similar goals for decarbonizing fleets. This is one of our fastest sites we've ever moved on, going from zero EVs to 100% electrified with over 60 EV route trucks by the end of the year. What this allows us to do is work with the utility one time, put the infrastructure in one time, go through a lot of training one time, and really upgrade the fleet for the employees all at once.

<!-- timestamp: 03:47 -->

**Christian Kreipke:** We purposely chose to build this thing on essentially the same platform as the gas vehicle, so that like for the upfit that needs to go into these, there'd be no guesswork. Secondly, was so that our drivers didn't have a completely new experience. We wanted them to really understand what is EV, but without the hurdle of a brand-new product they've never seen before. So like the Chassis Cab Cutaway, that's a great example of where we just took our already well-known, rock-solid vehicle and now made it EV, so that all of the like safety, all of the durability issues that go along with those vehicles was already done.

**Shane Doehrmann:** Our relationship at Utilimaster with Frito-Lay and Pepsi, that dates back to 1985. **Robert Ryals:** They didn't lose a lot of space on the interior, but they lost a lot of space on the exterior. The reverse cameras that they have in the old trucks are either on the side where they have to look all the way across the cab. This is center, center of the cab. Almost 13 inches of space, it's a full screen, you can see everything in it. The mirrors on the side are full vision, aligned. And they also have spots around the vehicle that can tell, it's sensors for anything that's real close to you and they come on real quick.

**Pedro Andrade:** What this EV platform gives us is the ability to add ADAS, blind spot monitoring system, collision mitigation systems. So all these features that overall make the vehicle safer for them, safer for the public, and just much more enjoyable to go to market.
