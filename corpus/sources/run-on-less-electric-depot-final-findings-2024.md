**Speaker (unclear):** NACFE and RMI have now completed our fourth Run On Less demonstration and reporting.
This one was called Run On Less - Electric Depot, and the major question we were trying to answer was, what does it take to scale electric trucks?

The effort began in late 2022 when we scoped the project, gathered sponsors, and then announced the Fleet Depot participants in April of 2023.

This was followed by a 10-episode educational bootcamp and visits to the 10 depots in June and July, where 122 people were interviewed and the truck, charging, and site details documented.

August brought depot profile videos and details from each site on RunOnLess.com.

The 3-week run took place in September, where 22 trucks were monitored for 18 days.

A detailed data set was publicly shared for anyone to dig into how the trucks were operated and charged.

On November 7th, NACFE released initial findings, just ahead of a four-hour visit to the White House that included nearly all the fleets and Mike Roeth and Dave Mullaney of NACFE and RMI.

In the next eight months, the team attended an industry event or two nearly every week to share the findings and gain new insights and understandings for the scaling of commercial electric trucks.

Most notably, NACFE held a Run On Less - Electric Depot data workshop, where 15 organizations shared details of analyses they performed using the data.

Now, two reports have been published.

Key findings include:
There have been big improvements in trucks and chargers since Run On Less - Electric in 2021.

Small energy depots are ready for electrification now and large energy depots are gaining momentum.

Energizing the sites takes too long.

Another interesting finding is that the diversity, passion, and capability of the people involved in this transformation is helping scale electric trucks.

The manufacturers of these vehicles should make cost and weight improvements a priority.

And finally, more realistic data on all key performance metrics is needed.

We would like to thank all the participants, sponsors, and everyone involved with this effort.

We have begun planning our fifth Run On Less for 2025 and cannot wait to announce our plans and bring another effort.

Here again, we will answer the biggest questions for our future of zero-emission freight movement.

Stay tuned.
